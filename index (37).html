How to download a YouTube thumbnail

  1. Open YouTube and copy the link of the video you need.
  2. Paste the link into the box above.
  3. Select “Get Thumbnail” to preview the available image.
  4. Open the image, then save it to your device.

About this tool

This browser-based tool displays publicly accessible thumbnail image files associated with a YouTube video. Image sizes depend on what the video makes available. Use thumbnails only when you have the right to use them, and respect the creator’s copyright and YouTube’s terms.
